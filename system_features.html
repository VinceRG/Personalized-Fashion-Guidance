Body Shape Analysis
Find flattering silhouettes designed specifically for your body proportions.

Hourglass

Balanced bust and hips with a defined waist.

Characteristics:

  • Bust and hips nearly equal width
  • Well-defined waistline
  • Balanced proportions

Styling Tips:

  • Wrap dresses to accentuate waist
  • Belted styles
  • Fitted silhouettes
✓ Your Shape

Pear

Hips wider than bust with a defined waist.

Characteristics:

  • Hips wider than shoulders
  • Defined waistline
  • Smaller upper body

Styling Tips:

  • A-line skirts and dresses
  • Boat necklines
  • Detailed tops to balance

Rectangle

Balanced body with minimal waist definition.

Characteristics:

  • Straight silhouette
  • Shoulders and hips equal
  • Minimal waist definition

Styling Tips:

  • Peplum tops to add curves
  • Belted dresses to create definition
  • Layering and texture

Inverted Triangle

Broader shoulders than hips with slimmer lower body.

Characteristics:

  • Broader shoulders and chest
  • Narrow waist and hips
  • Slender legs

Styling Tips:

  • Wide-leg pants and skirts
  • Horizontal stripes below
  • Bright bottoms

Key Styling Principles for Pear Shape

Do:

  • Emphasize your shoulders and neckline
  • Choose A-line or flared bottoms
  • Opt for darker colors on bottom
  • Add volume to your upper body

Avoid:

  • Tight-fitting pants without balance on top
  • Heavy embellishments on hips
  • Tapered or skinny ankle pants
  • Horizontal stripes on lower body
